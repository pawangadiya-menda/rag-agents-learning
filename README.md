## 🧠 PM Lens Philosophy

Every folder contains two things:
1. **Working code** — functional, clean, runnable
2. **Product thinking** — brief, insight, or decision doc that 
   explains *why* the technical choices were made

This is what separates a PM who understands AI from one who 
just reads about it.
